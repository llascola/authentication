# NNNN. Short title of the decision

- Status: Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by [NNNN](NNNN-...md)
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What forces are at play? What problem or question are we deciding on? Keep it to
the facts and constraints that actually drove the choice.

## Decision

The choice we made, stated in active voice: "We will ...". Be specific enough
that a reader can tell whether a given piece of code follows it.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, what we now have to watch out for.
Include the trade-offs we knowingly accepted and any follow-up this creates.
